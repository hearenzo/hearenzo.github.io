"The Reciting Machine"

2026 · 읊는 기계

Dates: 26 November – 9 December 2026

Location: Culture Station Seoul 284 (former Seoul Station)

Programme: unfoldX 2026 — Seoul Metropolitan Government & Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture

Medium: AI multi-agent, sound installation, modular synthesizer, Seoul Station archival sound, multichannel sound, display, mixed media, dimensions variable

The old Seoul Station is a building that was made to be left. When the trains moved to the new station in 2004, the only thing that left the building was its function as a station. The situation of its sound is stranger still. Sort the newsreels and cultural films about Seoul Station collected for this work, and two thirds of the sound is not the sound of events but narration — people reciting. The sound of the site itself surfaces only rarely in this material.

The Reciting Machine sits on top of these two absences. Three AI agents, each holding a different measure of what music is, share a single modular synthesizer, and for the whole run of the exhibition they cut, splice and erase the sound the state once recited. One searches the archive and wants the source of a sound to be heard. One listens to what is ringing in the room and wants it to slip free of its source and become music. One reads the people who have just walked into the room.

There is no conductor and no score. The three judgements collide and push against one another, and that process is itself the performance, sounding through the speakers. The sound made this way belongs to no one. When a sound no one ever made fills the room the station has left, the only ones who hear it are the visitors standing there at that moment.

System co-design and development: Byeongju Kim
