"The Reciting Machine"

2026 · 읊는 기계

Read the short visitor guide →

Dates: 26 November – 9 December 2026

Location: Culture Station Seoul 284 (former Seoul Station)

Programme: unfoldX 2026 — Seoul Metropolitan Government & Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture

Medium: Sound installation with a multi-agent AI system, modular synthesizer, archival recordings of Seoul Station, multichannel sound, display and mixed media. Dimensions variable.

Old Seoul Station was built for departures. In 2004 the trains moved to a new station. The building stayed where it was, emptied of its purpose as a station. What survives on the soundtracks is stranger still. Two thirds of the sound in the newsreels and cultural films I collected about Seoul Station is narration. The sounds of events at the station itself are rarely heard.

The Reciting Machine begins with these two absences. Three AI agents share a modular synthesizer. Each has a different idea of music. Throughout the exhibition they cut, splice and erase the sounds through which the state once told the station’s story. One searches the archive, wanting us to hear where a sound came from. Another listens to the sound in the room, wanting it to break free of its source and become music. The third watches the people who are there now.

There is no conductor and no score. The performance takes shape as the three agents’ choices clash, one giving way to another. The sound that comes through the speakers belongs to no one. A sound no one has made before fills the room the station left behind. The only people who hear it are the visitors standing there at that moment.

System co-design and development: Kim Byeongju
