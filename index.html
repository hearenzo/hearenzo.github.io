The Reciting Machine

unfoldX 2026 · 26 November – 9 December 2026 · Culture Station Seoul 284

Three AI agents, each holding a different measure of what music is, share a single modular synthesizer. For the whole run of the exhibition they cut, splice and erase the narration the state once recited over Seoul Station. There is no conductor and no score, and the sound that results belongs to no one.

about

Myungseok Oh (b. 1995, Seoul) is a sound artist and artistic researcher working in sound installation and live electronics. His work moves away from playing sound back, and toward exciting materials and systems that already exist so that they come to play themselves. Biography →
